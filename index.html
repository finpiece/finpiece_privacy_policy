FinPiece Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Summary

FinPiece (app ID com.finpiece.win) is a personal-finance app that works fully offline. It has no server and no online account.

Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal or financial data, and we cannot see it.

What FinPiece stores on your phone

  • Credit cards: nickname, bank name, the last 4 digits (never the full card number), limits, bills and payments (each payment with its date and time, the date you paid, the portal or app you paid through, the transaction or reference ID and an optional note you write).
  • Insurance policies: company, policy name and number, dates, premiums, cover and your notes, and each premium you mark as paid (with the same payment details as a card payment).
  • Expenses you add, with their categories.
  • Given-got: the name and mobile number of each person you choose, and the money given or got.
  • My Fin strategies and FIRE plan: the numbers you type (income, expenses, EMIs, savings, cover and similar).
  • To-Do: the tasks, short descriptions, sections and reminder times you write.
  • Doc Vault: the notes you write and the files you choose to add (any type, up to 10 MB each). Each file is encrypted before it is saved, kept in FinPiece’s private storage on your phone (not in your gallery, Files app or cloud backups), and opened only after your fingerprint, face unlock (Face ID on iPhone) or MPIN. FinPiece uses the phone’s own file picker, so it can read only the file you pick and needs no storage permission. A file you share from Doc Vault leaves the app as a normal, readable copy.
  • Your plan: when your free trial started, whether FinPiece Premium is active as Google Play or the App Store reports it (with its purchase date, whether it renews, and the store’s purchase reference for it), and, on the free plan, which items you chose to keep free and when you agreed to that.
  • Settings: reminder preferences, when FinPiece asks to unlock again, which dashboard tiles are open, your timezone, language, light / dark mode and an optional profile picture.

How your data is protected

  • All data is kept in one encrypted file (XChaCha20-Poly1305) in FinPiece’s private storage on your phone.
  • It can only be opened with your MPIN together with a secret key held in your phone’s secure hardware (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Your MPIN itself is never stored.
  • The data is excluded from Android backups and from iCloud / computer backups, so it never leaves your phone that way.
  • Backup file (optional, only when you ask for it in My account › Backup & new phone): FinPiece makes one encrypted file of your data and Doc Vault files, and you choose where it goes - a folder on your phone, Google Drive, email or another app. FinPiece sends it nowhere by itself. Only your recovery key can open it; without the key the file is unreadable. You use it to move your data to a new phone.
  • An optional recovery key (shown once, kept by you) can open your data if you forget your MPIN.
  • Doc Vault files are each encrypted with the same protected key before they are saved, kept in FinPiece’s private, no-backup storage (never in your gallery, Files app or cloud backups), and Doc Vault asks for your fingerprint, face unlock (Face ID on iPhone) or MPIN every time it is opened.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Contacts: on Android, FinPiece asks for no contacts permission. When you choose a person in Given-got, your phone’s own contact picker opens and gives FinPiece only the name and number you pick; the rest of your address book stays out of reach. On iPhone, contacts access is optional and asked only when you choose a person. Either way, only the person you pick is saved, inside FinPiece on your phone. Your address book is never copied or uploaded.
  • Photos (optional): only to pick a profile picture. The picture you choose is cropped and saved small, inside FinPiece.
  • Notifications (optional): for card bill, insurance premium and To-Do reminders, created on your phone.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock (Face ID on iPhone, optional): for quick login and to confirm it’s you before opening Doc Vault or exporting data. The check is done by your phone; FinPiece never receives fingerprint or face data.
  • Files (Doc Vault): FinPiece uses your phone’s own file picker. It needs no storage permission and can read only the file you pick; your original file is not moved or changed. When you open or share a Doc Vault file, the app you choose gets a temporary copy of that one file.
  • Saving a PDF to a folder (Android): when you save a Given-got statement or a card’s PDF, your phone’s own folder picker opens the first time and you choose a folder (for example Download). FinPiece remembers that choice and can only add files to that one folder; it cannot see or read your other files and needs no storage permission. On iPhone, the share sheet is used instead.
  • Google Play billing (Android): lets the Google Play app on your phone sell and renew FinPiece Premium. FinPiece itself still has no internet access; Google Play does the network part. If a payment is still being processed (for example a UPI payment), FinPiece asks the Google Play app on your phone every few hours, in the background, until the purchase is confirmed; nothing else is sent.
  • FinPiece does not request camera, microphone, location, SMS, storage or internet access.

Sharing

FinPiece contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party services that collect data.

FinPiece Premium (the yearly subscription) is bought, paid, renewed and cancelled through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone. Google or Apple handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; FinPiece never sees your card, UPI or bank details. On your phone, FinPiece only asks the store whether your subscription is active. None of your FinPiece data is sent to Google or Apple for this.

Data leaves the app only when you choose to send it: when you export a PDF (you choose the sections; Doc Vault and its files are included only if you switch them on and confirm it’s you) and pick where to save or share it; when you save one person’s Given-got statement as a PDF (with or without the “For what” notes, as you choose); when you save a card’s PDF (its details, bills and payments), which FinPiece asks you to do before you delete a card; when you open or share a Doc Vault file with another app; or when you choose to send a Given-got message on WhatsApp. An exported PDF or a shared file is a normal, readable copy, and what happens to it next is governed by the app or service you choose.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can edit or delete individual records at any time.
  • My account › Delete my account & data removes everything, including every Doc Vault file, immediately and permanently, after you confirm with your MPIN.
  • Deleting a credit card removes the card, its bills and its payments from FinPiece, after you have saved its PDF.
  • PDFs you have saved to a folder or shared are your own files, outside FinPiece: deleting a card, your data or the app does not remove them. Delete them yourself if you no longer want them.
  • Uninstalling FinPiece or factory-resetting your phone also deletes all FinPiece data.
  • On the free plan, data above its limits is kept on your phone (locked), never deleted. Deleting your FinPiece data does not cancel a FinPiece Premium subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions (Android) or Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions (iPhone).
  • Because we have no server, we hold no copy of your data: we cannot view, restore or delete it on your behalf.

Your rights

You are in full control: you can see all your data in the app, export the sections you choose as a PDF, correct it by editing, and erase it with Delete my account & data.

Children

FinPiece is meant for adults (18 and over) managing their own finances. It is not directed at children.

Financial information

The calculators and rules of thumb in FinPiece (such as My Fin strategies and the FIRE plan) are for education only. They are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and published at the same web address, with a new effective date.
